01 / Game guide

How to Play

The aim

Choose one actor or actress, then bid on films from that star's catalog to build a lineup you like. The game shows a film poster and title for each auction. Players decide for themselves which final lineup is best; Movie Draft does not calculate an official winner.

Choose a mode

  • Play online: The host creates a room for 2–5 players, chooses the star and settings, and shares its link or code. Guests enter a display name and join. The lobby shows who is connected. The host may start when everyone is present; the game can also start automatically shortly after the room fills.
  • Local two-player: Two people use the controls on one device.
  • Versus AI: One person plays against the computer opponent.

Settings

Each player starts with the same fictional budget from ₹20 to ₹100, in ₹10 steps (₹20 by default). Each player drafts 5, 6, or 7 films (5 by default). The host or local players can choose a film order where the catalog supports it. Film order may be shuffled, and films can reappear later when more auctions are needed.

Bidding

  1. The opening bid on a film is ₹1. Players can raise the displayed bid by ₹1 or use the +₹2 control, within their remaining game budget.
  2. A player cannot bid when their lineup is full or when the current film is ineligible for that player. A player cannot outbid their own leading bid.
  3. Teesko! Pass withdraws a player from that film's auction. In online play, a player who passes cannot bid again on that film.
  4. In an online room, when nobody else is eligible and able to raise the high bid, the high bidder gets the film for that amount. Some uncontested films can be claimed for ₹0. If nobody bids, a film can be skipped.
  5. The online host can skip a film. A film may appear again in a later auction. It can be drafted by different players; repeats within one player's lineup are normally prevented unless the star's whole catalog has fewer films than the selected lineup size.

The local two-player and AI controls resolve passes slightly differently from online rooms. The on-screen controls and messages govern the current round.

Finish and share

The draft ends when every player has filled their lineup. The result shows each lineup, amounts spent and game budget left. You can create a result image, download it, or use your device's share menu. If you post it to Instagram and ask friends to comment on the best lineup, that discussion takes place on Instagram, outside Movie Draft. There is no in-game vote, ranking, prize or official result.
